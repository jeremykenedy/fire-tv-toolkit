These are real filmed scenes with fixed recorded animals and camera movement. Scene selection changes the recording, and playback speed changes its timing. There are no independent density, species, simulated light-ray, camera, or depth controls. Legacy density, species, and light_rays preference values remain stored for compatibility, but have no visual effect in filmed mode. Random choices affect only the scene and playback speed. Loop crossfades are editorial transitions, not seamless natural events.
