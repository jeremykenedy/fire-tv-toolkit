### Jellyfish Drift capture and media

The Deep ocean image is an actual 1920 by 1080 non-test Dream capture from Jellyfish Drift 2.0.0/code4 on a physical API30 Fire TV, after a 40-second settle at 0.9x playback. Source footage: NOAA Ocean Exploration, 2016 Deepwater Exploration of the Marianas, observed at Enigma Seamount on 24 April 2016. NOAA identifies this material as public domain. No text or overlays were added. See the [media notice](../art/screenshots/jellyfish-media-notice.txt) and [tagged capture provenance](https://github.com/jeremykenedy/jellyfish-drift/blob/v2.0.0/art/screenshots/sources.json).
