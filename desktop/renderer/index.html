    Fire TV Toolkit

    Online television and practical tools for your Fire TV, in one place.

    What it does

    Browse worldwide public IPTV channels, save favorites and watch online streams. Connect a Fire TV to install TV apps, choose screensavers and home screens, and manage Alexa wake, idle timers and protection.

    Where it works

    The desktop app is built for macOS on Intel and Apple silicon, Windows x64 and Linux x64. TV tools are intended for Fire TV devices that allow ADB debugging. They were checked on an Insignia Fire TV Edition running Android 11. Other Fire TV models and Android TV devices may work, but features depend on the device and operating system.

    Its purpose

    This is a setup and playback tool, with changes you choose and review. It does not host or provide media, unlock paid channels, bypass subscriptions or act as a personal media server. Public streams may stop working, require regional access or be unavailable on your device.

    Use with care

    The app is designed for deliberate, reviewable changes. It does not intentionally damage devices, but changing system settings, uninstalling TV apps or running shell commands can have lasting effects. Some changes can be restored using the captured values; recovery cannot be guaranteed.
